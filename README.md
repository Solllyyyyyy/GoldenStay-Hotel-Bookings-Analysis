# Project Overview
Golden Stay Hotel is a customer focused hospitality brand based in Abuja, with two branches located in Maitama and Wuse 2 serving business and leisure travelers, committed to delivering comfort, service quality, operational excellence, and to ensure consistent guest satisfaction and sustainable business growth.

The hotel experiences revenue fluctuations, customer satisfaction, and booking patterns that affect occupancy and profitability. Without structured analysis, performance trends are unclear. This project addresses the need for organized data processing and clear performance insight.


## Dataset
The dataset had a total of 700 bookings and 16 fields

[GoldenStay_Hotel_Bookings_2024 (1).xlsx](https://github.com/user-attachments/files/33183576/GoldenStay_Hotel_Bookings_2024.1.xlsx)


## Objectives
* **What drives our revenue?**
Which booking channel generates the most sales?
Is revenue growth driven more by booking volume or room pricing?
During which month does the hotel achieve peak profitability?
 
* **Where are we losing profitability?**
Which room type has high revenue but low profit?
 
* **When is demand strongest and weakest?**
Which day category (weekday or weekend) records lower & higher booking volume?

* **Who are our most valuable customers?**

## Tools Used
* MySQL
* PowerBI

## Key Insights
*Business relies heavily on weekday demand.
*High-value revenue is concentrated in Suite rooms.
*Despite strong revenue, customer satisfaction score is low, which may affect retention.
*Revenue distribution across channels is fairly balanced, but Corporate underperforms

## Recommendations
* **Strengthen Weekend Demand:**
Introduce weekend packages (family, couple, staycation offers) to reduce weekday–weekend imbalance. Partner with event planners and wedding venues. 

* **Improve Customer Satisfaction:**
Strengthen service quality, housekeeping standards, and response time. Implement post-stay feedback follow-ups to improve experience.

* **Maximize High-Profit Room Types:**
Maintain premium pricing to protect brand positioning. Offer value-added perks instead of price cuts (airport pickup, executive lounge access). Use slightly flexible pricing for Standard/Deluxe during low-demand periods. 
Market Standard rooms for short-stay travelers and promote Deluxe rooms as “affordable luxury"

* **Month Trend:**
For weaker months, run targeted corporate promotions in March/April and May/July.
Introduce early-bird conference packages
